API key for developers

Verify your email to get a sg_k_… key right away, used as a bearer token on /api/hash to skip the anti-bot check (HMAC, timestamp, and per-IP limits remain unchanged).

What we store: your email, the chosen provider, and the issue date — solely to issue the key, moderate its use (warning and revocation in case of abuse), and, at your request, delete them. We don't keep the provider's token, we don't create a session, we don't do marketing. Details in the privacy policy.
