Usage tiers and terms

Who can attest, how many works per month, and how long the certificate stays retrievable from our archive. Clear terms, written before you need them.

The guarantees that are the same for everyone

Whatever tier you use, four things never change:

  • Verification is always free and unlimited, for anyone — even for those who have never attested anything. A certificate has value because anyone can check it, without paying and without registering.
  • The proof of existence never expires. Your work's fingerprint, the Bitcoin anchoring, the certificate's permanent page, and the signature verification remain valid forever, in every tier. What varies between tiers is only how long we keep a copy of the PDF (the "recover a lost certificate" feature) — the certificate you download is yours and never stops working.
  • No retroactive effects. Certificates issued before these terms were published remain retrievable with no expiry.
  • Privacy doesn't change. Basic use remains at zero personal data; an identity (your email) comes into play only in the tiers that need it to work — and only for that.

Basic — free and anonymous

This is the service you'll find on the homepage, just as it's always been: no registration, no personal data, your work never leaves your device. There's no nominal quota: there are anti-abuse protections (anti-bot checks and rate limits) designed to stop automation, not people.

Developer — free, with verified email

To integrate attestation into your code or your AI agent: confirm your email with Google, Microsoft, or LinkedIn and receive an API key with 50 attestations per month. Everything is self-service, all described on the developer page.

This tier is meant for building and testing an integration, not for running it in production — that's why the quota is deliberately low. If your application is (or is about to go) into production, the right tier is Professional: same email-based identity, a larger quota, a longer retention guarantee.

Professional — for those who attest regularly

A lightweight annual subscription for those who produce works regularly: 200 attestations per month and retention of the certificate copy for at least 5 years. No account and no password: your verified email is your identity, and you can attest directly from the website, just as you do today. Until 31 December 2026 it's available at a promotional price of 20 € per year (list price 24 €) — full details on the Professional tier page. Activate and manage it from your profile page — subscription status, this month's usage, the certificate archive, and the top-up log are always there, updated in real time.

Convention — for academies, institutions, and businesses

The institution signs an agreement and registers its email domains: from that moment, anyone with an address on that domain (students, teachers, staff) attests with their own email, and usage is deducted from the organization's monthly pool, with a summary per member. No key to distribute, no technical skill required from individuals. To start an agreement, write to it@spaziogenesi.org.

Once the pool is exhausted, members can still continue attesting in the Basic tier (free, with 6-month retrieval): the service is never denied, only the tier changes.

This also applies to software companies: if your app attests on behalf of your customers, an agreement gives you a dedicated monthly pool, and your users remain anonymous to us. Contact us for a quote: it@spaziogenesi.org. Applications that integrate attestation — with or without an agreement — can apply to the public Integrations showcase.

What "PDF retrieval" means

When you attest a work, you download the PDF certificate: that's yours, forever, and it stays verifiable forever. We archive a copy of it so you can re-download it if you lose it (the "recover certificate" feature). The length of this retention is what changes between tiers. Even after it expires, the work's proof of existence — fingerprint, date, Bitcoin anchoring, permanent page — remains public and verifiable with no time limit.

Honesty about the durations. The retention periods shown are guaranteed minimums, not deletion dates: today we keep all copies with no expiry, and certificates already issued will never be retroactively subject to limits introduced later. How personal data is handled in the email-based tiers is described in the privacy policy; every transparency commitment is documented in the Trust Center.
